Questions

Before you book

05 / 06
How fast is delivery?

Ten to fourteen working days depending on scope. Previews inside seventy-two hours.

Do I get the raw files?

Retouched selects are included in every package. Raw files on request for ₦50,000.

Do you travel to Lagos?

Yes. Travel and accommodation are billed at cost on top of the rate.

What's the deposit?

Sixty percent to hold a date. Non-refundable inside seven days of the shoot.

Who owns the images?

You get full usage rights. We retain portfolio rights unless you opt out in writing.

Do you reshoot?

Once, free of charge, if the fault is ours.
